Frequently asked questions

Is my city or town listed?

If your city is not listed, online tuition still works from anywhere. Message us on WhatsApp and we will try to help.

Can I get a home tutor in every city listed here?

Home tutor availability depends on the teachers registered in and around your area at the time. Online tutors can teach students from any city.

Do you have tuition centres in these cities?

No. Althea Scholar is an online platform connecting students and teachers — classes happen at home, online, or as agreed with the teacher.

Is registration free?

Yes, registration is free for students and teachers, and there is a free demo class before you commit to a teacher.
