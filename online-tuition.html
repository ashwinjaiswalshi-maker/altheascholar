Online Tuition Classes

Learn from a qualified teacher over live video — from home, at a time that suits your family. Register free and try a demo class before you commit.

Find a Tutor →Become a Teacher

Online tuition lets a student learn from a teacher over a live video call, from anywhere — including outside India. It removes the biggest limitation of home tuition (finding a good teacher physically nearby) while keeping the personal, one-on-one or small-batch format that makes tuition effective in the first place.

Many parents choose online tuition simply for convenience: no travel for the teacher, no rescheduling because of traffic or weather, and a schedule that works around school pickups and other children. Althea Scholar is an online platform that connects students with teachers — we help you find a suitable teacher, and you can start with a free demo class.

Benefits of online tuition

Learn from home

No commute for the student or the teacher — more time for learning and rest.

A wider choice of teachers

You are not limited to teachers who live near you, which matters most for less common subjects.

Flexible timing

Pick days and slots that fit school hours, homework and family routines.

Good for NRI and relocating families

Students abroad or moving cities can stay connected to Indian boards and syllabi.

Useful for exam preparation

For entrance and competitive exams a specialist teacher often matters more than proximity.

Frequently asked questions

Is online tuition suitable for younger children?

It can work well with shorter sessions and an engaging teacher, but some young children do better with in-person attention. Try a free demo class and, if needed, choose home tuition or both modes.

Is the demo class free?

Yes — Althea Scholar offers a free demo before you commit to a teacher. See the Find Tutor form to get started.

Which apps are used for classes?

Whichever you and the teacher are comfortable with — commonly Zoom, Google Meet or WhatsApp video.

Can I get an online teacher for any subject?

You can request any subject; whether a teacher is available depends on registered teachers for that class, subject and timing. Registration does not guarantee a match.

Can my child attend from abroad?

Yes. Online classes work from anywhere with a stable internet connection, and many families abroad use them to stay connected to Indian boards.

I want to teach online — how do I join?

Register free via Become a Teacher. The team reviews your documents before your profile is listed.
