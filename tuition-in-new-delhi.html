Looking for a reliable tutor in Delhi? Althea Scholar is an online platform that helps students and parents in Delhi and the wider NCR connect with teachers for home tuition and online tuition — from Nursery/KG through Class 12, across major boards, plus entrance exam preparation and hobby & language classes.

We're an online matching platform, not a walk-in tuition center — you can register from anywhere in and around Delhi, and we help you find a suitable teacher. Fee details and our platform policy are shown on the registration form; registration does not guarantee a teacher assignment.

Home tuition in Delhi

Home tuition means a teacher visits your residence on agreed days. In a large, spread-out region like Delhi NCR, the teacher's distance from your home is a practical factor, so we ask for your exact locality and consider it when suggesting a match. If distance is the obstacle, online classes remove it entirely.

Timings are decided with the teacher — many families prefer evening slots after school, while others book weekend or early-morning classes. Share your preferred days and times on the form.

Online tuition option

If a suitable home tutor isn't available near you, or you prefer learning from home without travel, online tuition is an alternative — the teacher and student meet on live video, so your exact locality in Delhi matters much less. Read more on our Online Tuition page, or pick Both as the tuition mode while registering.

Frequently asked questions about tuition in Delhi

Do you have a tuition centre in Delhi?

No. Althea Scholar is an online platform that connects students and teachers. Classes happen at the student's home, online, or as agreed with the teacher — we don't run a physical centre.

Can I get a home tutor in my locality in Delhi?

It depends on which teachers are registered for your class, subject and area. Mention your exact locality when you register and we will suggest suitable teachers if available.

Which boards do your teachers cover in Delhi?

You can request teachers for CBSE, ICSE and other boards. Tell us the board and class, and we will suggest teachers who teach that syllabus, if available.

I live in Gurugram / Noida / Ghaziabad — can I still register from this page?

Yes. These nearby areas are covered too, for both home and online tuition. Mention your exact area in the form.

Is the demo class free?

Yes, you can attend a free demo class before committing to a teacher.

I am a teacher in Delhi. How do I list myself?

Register free via Become a Teacher. Your registration is reviewed by the team, and once it is marked Verified a public profile can be created.
